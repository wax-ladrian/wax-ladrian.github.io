Hello all,

This post is not well structured, it is more of a general “these are some things I love and this is my attempt on a ‘thank you’ as well as my way to express ‘How did I get here?’”

On October 8th, 2021, the Narcissist Cookbook (the artistic name of Matt Johnson, one of my absolute favorite musicians) released an album called “This Is How We Get Better.” There are many different dimensions that contribute to me loving a specific song. A song may have “incredible lyrics” and also be very full of “interesting harmonies,” etc….   
  
I think, in terms of forming the person I am, music with wonderfully written lyrics are incredibly important, and on that category, “This Is How We Get Better” always comes immediately to mind. In that album (and not only on that album), Matt shows a level of vulnerability and understanding of human nature that makes it deeply resonate with me, no matter how many times I listen to. I can confidently say that I have listen to this album hundreds of times, and (of what I can remember) have cried at least twice to the music on it. I highly recommend you to listen to it, it is a wonderful piece of poetry wrapped in brilliant melody.

The first reason to write this email is to tell people to go listen to “This Is How We Get Better”^, the second reason is to tell you to go listen to the first (and probably only) release on Bandcamp with my name on it! First, a timeline:

- In Spring 2023 (?) I was introduced to the music of The Narcissist Cookbook for the first time by my friend Charlotte. She sent me some songs of their EP “Eden Disorder,” which is still her favorite album from tNC!
- In Summer 2023, I saw them live for the first time, and I have gone to see them live every summer since!
- In Spring 2024, I joined the MIT-Wellesley Toons, my favorite a capella group in all of reality
- Then, during the winter preceding my last semester in Toons, I decided I’d arrange one of their songs. I had two in mind but ended up arranging “Practically Imperfect” (and I also [performed it](https://youtu.be/Sg5uS-YIZIM "https://youtu.be/Sg5uS-YIZIM") with Toons!). I was a bit concerned because I had only arranged one song before (“Planting Flowers” by Tiny Habits, here is [the Toons performance](https://youtu.be/T1HB5evYuEc "https://youtu.be/T1HB5evYuEc")!) and it was so “bare bones” that people needed to step in and make it more singable (ty all so much for helping <3). I thought “Humm if I have the music sheet, maybe that will make it easier. And I’m in the tNC’s fan Discord, so I guess I should just reach out?!” I did not expect a reply, HOWEVER:
![](/images/Past20261013000320.png)That was just insane to begin with, so well, I went to do work on arranging it. I finished at some point (with help from Andres and Charlotte!!) and sent the mp3 file out of Musescore for them to listen to it, and……
![](/images/Past20261013000410.png)  
Fast forward to Summer 2025, their show in Boston (well, Cambridge) happened. The Toons had lunch at my apartment before the show, and we then went for sound check and to meet the man themself (apologies for the quality of the photo oops).
![](/images/Past20261013000411.png)

We got the green bracelets and had access to the venue at any time way before the audience had and it felt so cool to be on the BTS of things!

And then, finally, [the performance](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1vaEA4LAPn63Oob8C58Bj5eYF7c9U-s0U/view?usp=sharing "https://drive.google.com/file/d/1vaEA4LAPn63Oob8C58Bj5eYF7c9U-s0U/view?usp=sharing"). It was such a cool experience, I felt privileged but I also felt like I was returning at least a bit back to Toons after they have literally been one of the most wonderful things in my life. Giving them the chance to shine with an established artist was just amazing to witness and be part of! In that show, Matt said they would get us all together again to record a version of this song in a-capella at some point.

- Fast forward to Summer 2026, we all got together at a studio in Cambridge and did……. This!
